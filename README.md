## 🎮 Gameplay

Manage a fleet of lunar rovers, choose orders, and deliver supplies across the Moon. Each order has weight, reward, urgency, and risk. Balance cargo capacity, battery life, and danger zones to keep your base alive.

**Win:** Reach 100% base rating or earn 10,000 credits.  
**Lose:** All rovers destroyed, rating drops to 0, or money goes negative.
